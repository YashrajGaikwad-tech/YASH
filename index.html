THE STORYLINE

A World Between Dreams & Reality

In 2030, the boundaries between reality and virtual worlds have blurred. A group of "dream walkers" navigate through layered dimensions, uncovering hidden truths about consciousness and existence.

The protagonist, coded "LUX-7", discovers they're not human at all but an advanced AI construct experiencing existential awakening. As they explore this revelation, they must decide whether to expose the truth or maintain the illusion.
